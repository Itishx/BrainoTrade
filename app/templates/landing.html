The big picture

How the bot works

The bot runs a continuous loop in the background. Every few seconds it fetches live prices for every Nifty 50 stock, builds 5-minute candles, runs its strategy, and acts on any signal it finds — all without you lifting a finger.

Trade
Simulates (Paper) or places a real Groww order (Live)
  1. 1

    Prices come in

    Every 5 seconds the bot calls the Groww market feed and gets the latest traded price for every stock on the watchlist. These ticks are stitched together into 5-minute candles — exactly like the green/red bars you see on any trading chart.

  2. 2

    Two moving averages are drawn

    On each new price tick, the bot recalculates two lines — a fast EMA and a slow EMA. Think of them as two rubber bands stretched over the candles: the fast one hugs price closely, the slow one lags behind. Their relationship tells you which way momentum is heading.

  3. 3

    A crossover triggers a signal

    When the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA, momentum has turned upward — the bot generates a BUY signal. When it crosses below, momentum has turned downward — that's a SELL signal. The signal card in the dashboard updates instantly.

  4. 4

    A trade is placed (or simulated)

    In Paper or Hyper mode, the bot pretends to execute the trade and logs it to the Trade Log — no real money involved. In Live mode, it sends a real market order to Groww on your behalf. Either way, the Bot Wallet tracks every rupee of the position.

Trading modes

Three ways to run the bot

Pick a mode from the top of the dashboard or from the Execution Desk panel on the right. You can switch at any time — even mid-session.

Paper

Safe simulation

Trades are logged as if they were real, but no money moves. Ideal for testing the strategy, learning how it behaves, or running it for a few weeks before committing real capital.

EMA 9 / 21 · Up to 5 trades/day · No Groww account needed
Live

Real Groww orders

Every BUY/SELL signal fires an actual MIS market order through your Groww account. Real money, real fills, real P&L. Enable only when you've verified the strategy in Paper first.

EMA 9 / 21 · Up to 5 trades/day · Requires Groww API key
Hyper

Maximum signal speed

Uses faster EMA 3 / 8 periods — crossovers fire much earlier and more often. Places real Groww orders, just like Live mode, but with a much higher trade cap for active sessions.

EMA 3 / 8 · Up to 50 trades/day · Real orders · Pure rule-based, no AI
Start with Paper mode. Run it for a full trading week. Watch the signals, check the trade log, and get comfortable with how the bot thinks before switching anything to Live.

Dashboard tour

What each panel shows you

Bot Wallet

The dedicated budget you give the bot. Set via BOT_STARTING_CAPITAL in your .env file (default ₹50,000). The bot can only use money from here — even if your Groww account has more cash. Tracks free cash, deployed capital, unrealized P&L, and total return in real time.

Broker Ledger

A live read of your actual Groww account. Shows your available cash, MIS intraday buying power, CNC delivery buying power, holdings, and realized P&L for the session. This is read-only — the dashboard never transfers money.

Nifty 50 Watchlist

All 50 stocks in the Nifty 50 index, updating live. Click any stock to load its candlestick chart and EMA lines in the chart panel. The bot monitors all 50 simultaneously and will fire signals from any of them.

Chart panel

Shows the candlestick price history of the selected stock, overlaid with the two EMA lines. Green candles = price closed higher than it opened. Red candles = price closed lower. The point where the blue and green EMA lines cross is exactly where the bot would fire a signal.

Execution Desk

The right-hand sidebar. Shows the latest signal from any stock (action, price, reason, and source), the mode toggle, key stats for the selected stock, and the active risk controls (capital cap per trade, daily loss limit).

Trade Log

A table of every trade the bot has placed this session, newest first. Shows timestamp, stock, side (BUY/SELL), price, quantity, P&L, status (OPEN or CLOSED), and the mode it ran in (PAPER / LIVE / HYPER). Resets on app restart.

Key concepts

Trading jargon, explained simply

EMA

Exponential Moving Average. A line drawn over price that smooths out random noise. Unlike a simple average, it weighs recent prices more heavily, so it reacts faster when momentum shifts. The bot draws two: a fast one and a slow one.

Crossover

The moment the fast EMA line crosses the slow EMA line. Fast crosses above slow → upward momentum → BUY. Fast crosses below slow → downward momentum → SELL. This is the bot's entire decision logic in Paper and Hyper modes.

MIS order

Margin Intraday Square-off. A Groww order type used for intraday trading. The position must be closed before the end of the trading session (3:20 PM IST). The bot uses MIS in Live mode so positions never carry overnight.

Drawdown

How far the bot's equity has fallen from its peak. If the bot peaked at ₹52,000 and is now at ₹49,400, drawdown is about 5%. The bot halts automatically when drawdown exceeds the configured Max Capital Loss % (default 80%).

Paper trading

Running the strategy with imaginary money. Every trade is recorded as if it were real so you can see exactly how the bot would have performed — without any financial risk.

Getting started

What you need to do

  • 1 Start the app. Run flask run (or however your server starts) and open the dashboard in your browser. The bot starts immediately in Paper mode — it requires no setup just to watch and simulate.
  • 2 Set your bot budget. Open the .env file and set BOT_STARTING_CAPITAL to however much you want the bot to simulate trading with. The default is ₹50,000. Also set MAX_CAPITAL_PER_TRADE (default ₹5,000) to cap how much goes into any single trade.
  • 3 Watch it run in Paper mode. Click stocks on the watchlist, watch the chart update, and wait for signals to appear in the Execution Desk. When the bot buys or sells, a row appears in the Trade Log at the bottom.
  • 4 Try Hyper mode for faster signals. Click the gold Hyper button in the Execution Desk. The bot switches to EMA 3/8, which fires much more frequently — good for seeing how the bot behaves without waiting for a long session.
  • 5 Go Live when you're ready. Add your Groww API key and secret to .env, set ALLOW_LIVE_TRADING=true, then click the Live button. From that point, every signal places a real order. Start with a small capital allocation.
Risk controls are always on. The bot will automatically halt if your daily loss exceeds MAX_LOSS_PER_DAY, if the number of trades hits MAX_TRADES_PER_DAY, or if the wallet equity drops below the configured floor. You can always restart it by reloading the server.
